# Step 05: Translate Results

## Goal

Turn the validated estimate into a cautious, decision-ready summary.

## Report

- Method used: Bucher common-comparator, Bucher chain, MAIC, or anchored MAIC.
- Contrast orientation.
- Estimate, confidence interval, and analysis scale.
- Natural-scale estimate for log ratio measures.
- Input evidence sources.
- MAIC ESS and balance summary when applicable.
- Assumption caveats and fragility flags.

## Validation Checkpoint

The final summary must name the method, target contrast, evidence layout,
analysis scale, natural-scale estimate, and limitations. Do not present the
estimate as definitive comparative effectiveness evidence.

## Handoff

When the estimate informs future design work, hand it to
`vera-clinical-trial-designing` as a sensitivity assumption rather than a single
stand-alone point estimate.
